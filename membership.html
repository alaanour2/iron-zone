FAQ

Is there a contract?

Memberships run month to month and do not lock you into a long contract. The prices on this page are demonstration figures, so read them as an illustration of the offer rather than a live quote.

Can I cancel anytime?

In a real gym you would cancel at any time with no exit fee and keep access until the end of the month you paid for. This site is a demonstration, so the policy is only described here to show what the page would say.

Do I need experience to start training?

No. Most members start with the fundamentals, and your onboarding session covers the basics before you touch a barbell. If you have trained before, your plan simply begins at a higher level.

How many sessions a week should I attend?

Two to four sessions a week is where most members settle. Consistency matters more than the number: keep roughly the same slots each week and progress the loads slowly, rather than training hard for a few days and then stopping.

Is personal training included in every plan?

No. Personal training is part of the Elite plan. Starter includes full access to the gym floor and the group classes, Pro adds priority booking and nutrition guidance, and a coach can also be booked by the hour with any plan.

What should I bring to my first session?

Training clothes, flat shoes with a grippy sole, a water bottle, and a towel. We provide the equipment, but arrive ten minutes early for the onboarding and a quick tour of the floor.

What happens if I miss a few weeks?

Nothing breaks and nobody chases you. Come back, tell your coach where you stopped, and the plan continues from the sessions you logged. A broken streak is a normal part of training - the goal is to still be training a year from now.

When will I see results?

The first few weeks usually bring better technique and more stamina on the machines. Visible changes in strength and body composition take two to three months of consistent work, and they depend on nutrition and sleep as much as on the sessions themselves.
