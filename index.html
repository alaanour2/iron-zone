Welcome

INTRODUCING IRON ZONE

IRON ZONE is a premium fitness and personal training facility built around three simple ideas: serious equipment, certified coaches, and a plan for every member. Whether you are setting foot on the gym floor for the first time or chasing a new personal record, the space and the coaching are designed so that every single session counts.

The floor covers the full spectrum of strength work: free-weight platforms for barbell lifts, machine circuits for hypertrophy, dedicated cardio and functional zones, and a studio that hosts group classes throughout the week. Equipment is chosen for reliability rather than novelty, so the tools you depend on are always calibrated, maintained, and ready to use.

Every membership starts with an onboarding session. A coach reviews your goals, your movement history, and the time you can realistically give each week, then builds a training split you can actually sustain. From that point the plan evolves with your progress - through personal training, small-group classes, or self-directed sessions with our coaches on hand for technique checks and programming questions.

What We Offer

TRAINING BENEFITS

Training benefits are not a list of miracles. What follows are the results of a well-equipped floor, a plan you can follow, and coaches who tell you the truth about your numbers - measured over months, not weekends.

Strength Training

Progressive barbell and machine work that builds usable strength and adds lean muscle over months rather than weeks. Loads and rep ranges are set to match the level you are actually at today.

Personal Coaching

One coach, one written plan, and honest correction on the lifts that matter. The hour goes into technique and programme adjustments, not into empty encouragement.

Group Classes

Coach-led sessions in a small group. This is often the practical middle ground: the structure of a class, with just enough attention to correct your form as you go.

Cardio & Endurance

Steady zone-based cardio and circuit work that builds a base your body can actually hold on to - and that shortens the recovery time between the heavier sessions.

Mobility & Flexibility

Warm-ups, mobility work, and assisted stretching. Unglamorous, but it is the habit that keeps people training for years instead of months.

Functional Training

Carrying, pushing, pulling, and rotating with loads that match daily life - the part of fitness that shows up outside the gym, on stairs, with a heavy bag, or after a long day at a desk.

Results are easier to keep when you can see them, so every membership comes with a workout log that records your sessions locally in your browser - you and your coach can compare weeks instead of guessing.
